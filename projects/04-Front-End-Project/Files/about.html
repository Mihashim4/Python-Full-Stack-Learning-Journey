Our Story

This website is a tribute to my grandfather's old shop — HASHIM Auto Parts & Old Motor Spare Parts.

For many years, the shop was a trusted place in Marthandam where people could find new and old spare parts for cars, lorries, autos, and more. From engine parts to bearings, tires, and electrical components — my grandfather helped many customers keep their vehicles running.

Though the physical shop is no longer there, the hard work, honesty, and service it provided remain in our memories.

This website is created in loving memory of that small but important business that served the community for a long time.
